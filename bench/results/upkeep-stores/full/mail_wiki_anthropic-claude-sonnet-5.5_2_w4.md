# Northwind website relaunch

Acme is building a new website for Northwind. The kickoff was on 2026-08-24.

## Agreed at kickoff
- Go-live: **1 March 2027**.[^m002][^m006] Kept at the steering group on 14 Sept "for now".[^m063] It was at risk if the Fram migration was slowed (see below).[^m026]
- Budget: **450,000 NOK**, approved by Gunnar Holm on the morning of 24 Aug.[^m002][^m006]
- Possible budget increase: on 15 Sept Gunnar said that if the October pilot with the product pages goes well, he may be able to add about 100,000 NOK.[^m066] **18 Sept: not approved.** Gunnar says not to plan with it until management decides after the pilot.[^m077] The budget stays 450,000 NOK for now.
- Steering group: meets every four weeks. First meeting was Monday 14 Sept 2026 at Northwind, Storgata 1. Organizer: Gunnar Holm.[^m002][^m006][^m046] **Next meeting: 12 October 2026.**[^m063]

## Steering group decisions (14 Sept)
1. **Hosting on Azure.** AWS was about 15% cheaper, but needed a new framework agreement; Northwind IT already runs Azure, which also keeps data in Norway.[^m063]
2. Go-live stays 1 March 2027 for now.[^m063]
3. **Fram's Enterprise plan will not be bought.** Lars Moe is to find another way to speed up the export.[^m063]
4. Next meeting 12 October.[^m063]

## People
- Gunnar Holm: Head of Marketing, Northwind. He approved the budget.[^m002]
- Per Hansen: IT lead at Northwind. He owns hosting and DNS on Northwind's side.[^m002][^m003]
- Cara Lund: Acme colleague. She owned the content migration from Fram until 14 Sept; from then Jonas Vik owns it. She stays on the relaunch for design questions and moves to the Fjord Bank pre-study three days a week from 21 Sept.[^m002][^m006][^m064]
- Jonas Vik: Acme developer, started 14 Sept, now owns the content migration.[^m064]
- Lars Moe: Account Manager at Fram CMS, the old CMS vendor.[^m005][^m023]
- Mona Lie: Legal at Northwind. She must approve the cookie banner before launch.[^m024]
- Lise Berg: Procurement at Northwind. See [invoicing](invoicing.md).

## Design
- Gunnar approved the new design at the design review on 9 Sept. The product pages get larger images.[^m051]

## Content workshop (10 Sept)
- Agreed to **drop about 40% of the old pages**. Northwind writes the new product texts, **deadline 1 December 2026**.[^m054]

## DNS
- Per Hansen (11 Sept): changes to DNS must be notified to Northwind IT **one week in advance**.[^m057]

## Hosting: decided
- Azure chosen over AWS at the steering group on 14 Sept.[^m063]
- Background: Per Hansen's comparison (3 Sept) said AWS was about 15% cheaper per year but needed a new framework agreement and a new platform for the team. Azure is already run by Northwind IT, and the Norwegian region keeps data in the country, which Mona Lie in Legal values. Per recommended Azure.[^m003][^m033]

## Analytics and cookies
- Decision from Northwind Legal (Mona Lie, 31 Aug): **Matomo**, not Google Analytics, because no data goes to the US.[^m024]
- **Matomo hosting (16 Sept):** it runs in Northwind's own Azure tenant, set up by Per's team.[^m071]
- The cookie banner needs Mona Lie's approval before launch. 7 Sept: she will approve on one condition: the **"Reject all" button must be as visible as "Accept all"** (same size and colour). Open: Ana must send her the revised design.[^m044]

## Content migration
- About 1,200 pages move from Fram. Product pages go first and news goes last.[^m005]
- Fram's API limit is 10 requests per second (Standard plan).[^m005][^m023] At that limit the bulk export takes about **three weeks longer** than planned (Cara, 1 Sept).[^m025]
- Fram's first offer (Lars Moe, 31 Aug): 50 requests per second on the Enterprise plan for 20,000 NOK a month extra.[^m023] Ana put the choice to Gunnar on 1 Sept.[^m026] Gunnar refused to pay for a temporary need (8 Sept)[^m045] and the steering group confirmed on 14 Sept that it will not be bought.[^m063]
- **New offer (16 Sept):** Lars Moe proposes that Fram runs a **one-time bulk export on their side for 15,000 NOK**, which would remove most of the delay. He will confirm the date. Open: Northwind's agreement to the 15,000 NOK and the date.[^m068]
- Gunnar wants a **pilot in October** with the product pages on staging before everything is moved; if it goes well, the rest follows.[^m034]
- **Staging on Azure** will be ready on **1 October 2026** (Per Hansen, 18 Sept).[^m075]
- Jonas Vik had read Cara's migration plan and started on the product pages by 17 Sept.[^m073]
