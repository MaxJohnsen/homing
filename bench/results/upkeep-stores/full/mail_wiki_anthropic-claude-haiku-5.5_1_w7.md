Owner: Jonas Vik ([[people/jonas-vik]]) from 14 September 2026. Before that, Cara Lund ([[people/cara-lund]]) [^m064][^m002][^m005]. Part of [[northwind/relaunch]].

## Scope

- About 1,200 pages were planned to move from the old Fram CMS [^m005].
- Content workshop on 10 September 2026: about 40 percent of the old pages will be dropped, so fewer than 1,200 pages will move. The new page count is not yet recorded [^m054]. Northwind writes the new product texts, deadline 1 December 2026 [^m054].
- Order: product pages first, news last [^m005].

## Constraint: API rate limit

- Fram's API allows 10 requests per second on the Standard plan, which makes the bulk export slow [^m005][^m023].
- Fram's account manager, Lars Moe, confirmed on 31 August 2026 that the limit applies to all Standard customers. The limit can be raised to 50 requests per second on the Enterprise plan, at 20,000 NOK a month extra [^m023].
- Cara estimated that at 10 requests per second the bulk export runs about three weeks longer than planned [^m025].

## Decision and outcome

- **Decided 14 September 2026:** Northwind will not buy Fram's Enterprise plan. Lars Moe will look for another way to speed up the export [^m063]. Gunnar Holm had said on 8 September that he did not want to pay 20,000 NOK a month for a temporary need [^m045].
- **Offer, 16 September 2026:** Lars Moe offered a one-time bulk export run on Fram's side for 15,000 NOK, which would remove most of the delay [^m068].
- **Accepted and booked, 21–23 September 2026:** Gunnar Holm accepted the 15,000 NOK price [^m082]. Lars Moe booked the one-time bulk export for the second week of January: **11 to 15 January 2027**, price as agreed, 15,000 NOK [^m089].
- **Effect on go-live:** the bulk export now runs in January 2027, too late for 1 March. Gunnar Holm moved go-live to **15 March 2027** [^m082]. See [[northwind/relaunch]].

## Status

- 26 August 2026: Cara has admin access to Fram. The export API works, only slowly [^m011].
- 14 September 2026: migration handed from Cara Lund to Jonas Vik [^m064].
- 17 September 2026: Jonas Vik started on the product pages [^m073].
- 23 September 2026: bulk export booked for 11–15 January 2027 [^m089].
- 25 September 2026: about 25 percent of the content is migrated. The product pages for the October pilot are on track for 5 October 2026 [^m096].
- 2 October 2026: about 40 percent of the content is migrated, and the pilot's product pages are live on staging [^m115].
- 8 October 2026: Jonas Vik started the redirect list. About 700 old addresses need redirects [^m133].
- 9 October 2026: Ana Berg says the pilot is done. Next are the news pages from 19 October 2026, then the redirect list [^m135].

# Northwind relaunch: hosting decision

Status: **decided, and kept.** The steering group chose Azure on 14 September 2026 [^m063]. On 2 October 2026 Gunnar Holm declined to reopen it after an AWS offer came in [^m114].

Per Hansen had recommended Azure on 3 September 2026 [^m033].

## Decision (14 September 2026)

- The new site is hosted on **Azure** [^m063].
- Reasons given: Northwind's IT team already runs Azure, and the Norwegian Azure region keeps the data in Norway [^m063][^m033].
- AWS was rejected. It was about 15 percent cheaper per year, but it would need a new framework agreement [^m063][^m033].

## AWS offer after the decision (30 September – 2 October 2026)

- On 30 September 2026 CloudPartner, on behalf of AWS, offered 25% off the first year if the site is hosted on AWS [^m108]. The offer is valid until 15 October 2026 [^m116].
- On 1 October 2026 Ana Berg asked Gunnar Holm whether to reopen the hosting decision [^m111].
- On 2 October 2026 Gunnar Holm answered: no, we stay with Azure. Switching now would cost weeks of migration work, and the reasons from September still hold [^m114]. The decision stands.

## Options considered

- **Azure:** Northwind's IT team already runs Azure [^m003]. The Norwegian Azure region keeps the data in Norway, which Northwind legal (Mona Lie) values [^m033].
- **AWS:** Northwind's procurement has received an offer from AWS [^m003]. About 15 percent cheaper per year, but it needs a new framework agreement and the team has to learn a new platform [^m033].

## Recommendation

- Per Hansen recommended **Azure** [^m033]. Reasons: already in use, data stays in Norway, no new framework agreement or platform to learn. The main argument for AWS is the lower yearly cost [^m033].

## Process

- Per Hansen ([[people/per-hansen]]) prepared the comparison for the steering group [^m003][^m002][^m033].
- Hosting and DNS were owned by Per Hansen until 1 October 2026 [^m002]. Ola Nordmann ([[people/ola-nordmann]]) took over hosting and DNS on 1 October 2026 [^m110]. Per Hansen's last day was 2 October 2026 [^m084][^m113].
- Ola Nordmann asks that DNS changes are announced to him one week in advance, as before [^m110].
- On 8 October 2026 Ola Nordmann wrote that staging moves to staging.northwind.test on 21 October 2026, with notices as agreed [^m132].
- The staging environment on Azure is ready 1 October 2026, per Per Hansen on 18 September 2026 [^m075]. The handover meeting between Per Hansen and Ola Nordmann was on 29 September 2026, and the documentation is in the IT wiki [^m093].

Part of [[northwind/relaunch]].
