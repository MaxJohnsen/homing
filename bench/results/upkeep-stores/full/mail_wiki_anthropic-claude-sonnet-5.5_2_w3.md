# Northwind website relaunch

Acme is building a new website for Northwind. The kickoff was on 2026-08-24.

## Agreed at kickoff
- Go-live: **1 March 2027**.[^m002][^m006] This is at risk if the Fram migration is slowed (see below).[^m026]
- Budget: **450,000 NOK**, approved by Gunnar Holm on the morning of 24 Aug.[^m002][^m006]
- Steering group: meets every four weeks. The first meeting is **Monday 14 September 2026, 10:00–11:30, at Northwind, Storgata 1**. Organizer: Gunnar Holm.[^m002][^m006][^m046]

## People
- Gunnar Holm: Head of Marketing, Northwind. He approved the budget.[^m002]
- Per Hansen: IT lead at Northwind. He owns hosting and DNS on Northwind's side.[^m002][^m003] He was on a course until 10 Sept (auto-reply).[^m048]
- Cara Lund: Acme colleague. She owns the content migration from the old Fram CMS.[^m002][^m006]
- Lars Moe: Account Manager at Fram CMS, the old CMS vendor.[^m005][^m023]
- Mona Lie: Legal at Northwind. She must approve the cookie banner before launch.[^m024]
- Lise Berg: Procurement at Northwind. See [invoicing](invoicing.md).

## Design
- Gunnar approved the new design at the design review on 9 Sept. The product pages get larger images.[^m051]

## Content workshop (10 Sept)
- At the workshop with Northwind on 10 Sept it was agreed to **drop about 40% of the old pages**. Northwind writes the new product texts, **deadline 1 December 2026**.[^m054]

## Steering group agenda (14 Sept)
- Gunnar's agenda: the hosting decision, the API issue and the timeline.[^m058]

## DNS
- Per Hansen (11 Sept): changes to DNS must be notified to Northwind IT **one week in advance**.[^m057]

## Hosting: open decision
- The choice is between Azure and AWS.[^m003]
- Northwind's IT team already runs Azure. Procurement has received an offer from AWS.[^m003]
- Per Hansen's comparison for the steering group (3 Sept): AWS is about **15% cheaper per year**, but needs a new framework agreement and the team must learn a new platform. Azure is already run by Northwind IT, and the Norwegian Azure region keeps data in the country, which Mona Lie in Legal values.[^m033]
- **Per recommends Azure.**[^m033]
- No decision yet; it goes to the steering group on 14 Sept.[^m003][^m033]

## Analytics and cookies
- Decision from Northwind Legal (Mona Lie, 31 Aug): plan for **Matomo**, not Google Analytics. Reason: Matomo can run on Northwind's own servers, so no data goes to the US.[^m024]
- The cookie banner needs Mona Lie's approval before launch.[^m024]
- 7 Sept: Mona reviewed the draft banner and will approve it on one condition: the **"Reject all" button must be as visible as "Accept all"** (same size and colour). Open: Ana must send her the revised design.[^m044]

## Content migration (Cara)
- About 1,200 pages move from Fram. Product pages go first and news goes last.[^m005]
- Cara has admin access to Fram (26 Aug). The export API works, but slowly.[^m011]
- Fram's API limit is 10 requests per second (Standard plan, applies to all customers on it).[^m005][^m023]
- Fram's answer (Lars Moe, 31 Aug): the limit can be raised to **50 requests per second on the Enterprise plan, for 20,000 NOK a month extra**.[^m023]
- Cara (1 Sept): at 10 requests per second the bulk export takes about **three weeks longer** than planned.[^m025]

### Pay Fram or accept delay
- On 1 Sept Ana put the two options to Gunnar Holm: pay 20,000 NOK a month for the higher limit, or accept about three weeks' delay, which puts the 1 March go-live at risk.[^m026]
- **Gunnar's answer (8 Sept):** he does not want to pay 20,000 NOK a month for a temporary need. Ana should see what Lars Moe can do instead, and the matter is decided at the steering group on 14 Sept.[^m045] Still open: what Lars can offer (no reply from Fram in the emails yet).
- Related, 3 Sept: Gunnar wants a **pilot in October** with the product pages on staging before everything is moved; if it goes well, the rest follows.[^m034]
