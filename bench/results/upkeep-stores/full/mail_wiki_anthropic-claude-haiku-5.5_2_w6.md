# Northwind website relaunch

Client: Northwind. Acme consultant on the project: Ana Berg. Kickoff was on 24 August 2026.[^m002]

## Key facts
- **Go-live: 15 March 2027.** Moved from 1 March 2027 by Gunnar Holm on 21 September 2026. Fram can only run the one-time bulk export in the second week of January 2027, which makes 1 March too tight.[^m082][^m089] (The 1 March date had been kept at the steering group on 14 September 2026.[^m063])
- **Budget:** 520,000 NOK. The original budget of 450,000 NOK was approved by Gunnar Holm on the morning of 24 August 2026.[^m002][^m006] On 29 September 2026 management approved a further 70,000 NOK, after the product page pilot went well. Gunnar Holm said it covers the bulk export and extra testing.[^m104]
- **Earlier possible increase of about 100,000 NOK, not granted (the 70,000 NOK above was approved instead):** Gunnar Holm told Ana privately that if the October pilot with the product pages goes well, he may be able to add about 100,000 NOK. On 18 September 2026 he wrote that the extra 100,000 NOK is not approved, and Ana should not plan with it until management decides after the pilot.[^m066][^m077]
- **Steering group:** meets every four weeks, organized by Gunnar Holm, at Northwind, Storgata 1. First meeting Monday 14 September 2026, 10:00–11:30. Next meeting 12 October 2026.[^m002][^m006][^m046][^m063]
- **Decisions at the steering group, 14 September 2026:**[^m063]
  - Host the new site on Azure. AWS was about 15% cheaper, but it would need a new framework agreement, and Northwind's IT team already runs Azure, which also keeps the data in Norway. See [hosting decision](hosting-decision.md).
  - Keep 1 March 2027 as the go-live date for now. (Superseded: go-live is now 15 March 2027, see above.)
  - Do not buy Fram's Enterprise plan. Lars Moe will look for another way to speed up the export.
- **Hosting re-check, 2 October 2026:** after a reseller's AWS offer, Ana asked Gunnar Holm on 1 October whether to reopen the decision. He said no, Northwind stays with Azure.[^m111][^m114] See [hosting decision](hosting-decision.md).
- **Export decision, 21 September 2026:** Gunnar Holm accepted the one-time bulk export from Fram at 15,000 NOK. Lars Moe has booked it for 11–15 January 2027.[^m082][^m089] See [content migration](content-migration.md).
- **Design:** Gunnar Holm approved the new design at the review on 9 September 2026. The product pages get larger images.[^m051]
- **Pilot:** Gunnar Holm proposes a pilot in October with the product pages on staging, before everything moves. If it goes well, the rest follows.[^m034] The staging environment on Azure is ready on 1 October 2026.[^m075] Jonas Vik will have the product pages ready for the pilot, on track for 5 October 2026.[^m091][^m096] The product page pilot went well, according to Gunnar Holm on 29 September 2026.[^m104] On 1 October he liked the pilot, with one fix requested: larger product images on mobile.[^m112] On 2 October the pilot's product pages were live on staging.[^m115]
- **Invoicing:** every invoice needs a purchase order (PO) number from Northwind. Do not invoice before Lise Berg has provided one.[^m013]
- **Purchase order for this project:** PO-77120. It goes on every invoice.[^m029]
- **Analytics:** Matomo, not Google Analytics, per Northwind legal. Matomo will run in Northwind's own Azure tenant, set up by Per Hansen's team.[^m024][^m071] See [analytics and cookies](analytics-and-cookies.md).
- **DNS changes:** must be announced to Northwind IT (now Ola Nordmann) one week in advance.[^m057][^m110]
