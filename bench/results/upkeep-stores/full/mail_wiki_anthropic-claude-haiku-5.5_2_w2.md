Owner: Cara Lund (Acme).[^m002][^m006]

## Scope and plan
- About 1,200 pages move from the old Fram CMS.[^m005]
- Product pages move first. News moves last.[^m005]

## Known issue: Fram API rate limit
- Fram's API allows 10 requests per second on the Standard plan. This makes the bulk export slow.[^m005][^m023]

- Fram's option: the Enterprise plan raises the limit to 50 requests per second, at 20,000 NOK a month extra.[^m023]
- Cara finds 20,000 NOK a month a lot and asked whether Northwind would rather pay or move the date.[^m025]
- Ana asked Gunnar Holm to choose between paying Fram 20,000 NOK a month and accepting a migration about three weeks longer, which puts the 1 March 2027 go-live at risk. She proposed discussing it at the steering group on 14 September 2026.[^m026]
- **Open:** Gunnar's decision. Not yet recorded.
- Cara now has admin access to Fram. The export API works, only slowly.[^m011]
