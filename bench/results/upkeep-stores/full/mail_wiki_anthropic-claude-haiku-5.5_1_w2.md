# Northwind: content migration from Fram CMS

Owner: Cara Lund ([[people/cara-lund]]) [^m002][^m005]. Part of [[northwind/relaunch]].

## Scope

- About 1,200 pages move from the old Fram CMS [^m005].
- Order: product pages first, news last [^m005].

## Constraint: API rate limit

- Fram's API allows 10 requests per second on the Standard plan, which makes the bulk export slow [^m005][^m023].
- Fram's account manager, Lars Moe, confirmed on 31 August 2026 that the limit applies to all Standard customers. The limit can be raised to 50 requests per second on the Enterprise plan, at 20,000 NOK a month extra [^m023].
- Cara estimates that at 10 requests per second the bulk export runs about three weeks longer than planned, and she finds 20,000 NOK a month expensive [^m025].
- **Open decision:** pay Fram 20,000 NOK a month for the higher limit, or accept the three-week delay, which puts the 1 March 2027 go-live at risk [^m026]. Ana has asked Gunnar Holm to decide, and proposed discussing it at the steering group on 14 September 2026 [^m026]. No decision recorded yet.

## Status

- 26 August 2026: Cara has admin access to Fram. The export API works, only slowly [^m011].
- 31 August 2026: Fram's answer on the rate limit received (see above) [^m023].

# Northwind relaunch: hosting decision

Status: **open.** No choice made yet. Per Hansen recommends Azure (3 September 2026) [^m033].

## Options

- **Azure:** Northwind's IT team already runs Azure [^m003]. The Norwegian Azure region keeps the data in Norway, which Northwind legal (Mona Lie) values [^m033].
- **AWS:** Northwind's procurement has received an offer from AWS [^m003]. About 15 percent cheaper per year, but it needs a new framework agreement and the team has to learn a new platform [^m033].

## Recommendation

- Per Hansen recommends **Azure** [^m033]. Reasons: already in use, data stays in Norway, no new framework agreement or platform to learn. The main argument for AWS is the lower yearly cost [^m033].

## Process

- Per Hansen ([[people/per-hansen]]) prepared the comparison for the steering group on 14 September 2026 [^m003][^m002][^m033].
- Hosting and DNS are owned by Per Hansen [^m002].
